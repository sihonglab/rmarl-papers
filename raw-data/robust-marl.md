2020 ITSC, benchmarking paper, from Monash University. The conclusion is that the experimental results show that the proposed control method based on RL is robust in random traffic flow and changing traffic demand patterns, and is superior to the fixed time and vehicle driving methods. However, it is unstable in the case of high sensor data noise. Moreover, the trained RL-based controller can continuously learn online and improve its performance by interacting with the dynamic traffic environment, especially when there is traffic congestion and noisy sensor observations.
